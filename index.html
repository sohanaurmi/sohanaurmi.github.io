About

Farmers in Bangladesh are already living with climate change. The harder question is how quickly useful knowledge can reach them. They are the first to lose when the climate shifts, and often the last to learn how to protect their land and income. When farming stops paying, the nation's food security goes with it.

Conservation agriculture can change that. It protects the soil farmers live on, lowers what they spend and keeps their harvests standing through a changing climate. My research identifies what it takes for farmers to adopt it, and I share that knowledge with them directly. My goal is to build extensive expertise in this field and apply it through policy and international organisations, so that resilient farming reaches every farmer who needs it.
